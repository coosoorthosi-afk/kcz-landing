3. Right of withdrawal and why it does not apply

Consumers buying at a distance in Spain and the EU/EEA normally have a 14-day right of withdrawal. Article 103(m) of Royal Legislative Decree 1/2007 sets out an exception for digital content supplied without a tangible medium, where performance has begun.

That exception applies only where both of the following are true, and both are built into the checkout for this Course:

1.You give prior express consent for performance to begin immediately, before the withdrawal period has ended; and
2.You expressly acknowledge that, by doing so, you lose your right of withdrawal.

By completing your purchase you make both declarations. Your right of withdrawal therefore ends when access to the Course is delivered, which happens immediately.

Confirmation of that request and acknowledgement is sent to you by email as a durable medium, together with your receipt.
