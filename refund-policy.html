Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026 · Version 1.0

1. The short version

The Course is digital and delivered in full the moment you pay, so it is not covered by an unconditional return right. What applies instead is the Do-The-Work Guarantee below. If something is not working for you, write to me first: almost everything is solved within one working day.

2. The Do-The-Work Guarantee

I stand behind the system for the people who actually use it. You are entitled to a full refund if all of the following apply:

1.You request it within 14 days of purchase, by email from the address you used at checkout;
2.You have completed the first four modules of the Course; and
3.You attach the work you produced following them, showing that the system did not work for you even though you followed it.

The point of these conditions is to filter serial refunders, not honest students. If you did the work and it genuinely did not deliver, you get your money back with no hard feelings. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 10 working days.

3. What is not refundable

Purchases older than 14 days. Requests that do not include the completed work described above. Changing your mind, or not having the time to follow the Course. Not reaching a given number of followers, clients or income. Access terminated for breaching the Terms of Service, such as sharing or redistributing the material. Repeat purchases of a product already refunded once.

Third-party costs are also outside this policy, including Higgsfield credits and Adobe subscriptions, since they are paid to those providers and not to me.

4. What I do fix, always

✓A lesson that does not play, or access that is not working.
✓A duplicate or mistaken charge, refunded in full and without questions.
✓A module that goes out of date because a tool changed: it gets re-recorded and you get it free.

5. EU, EEA and UK consumers: right of withdrawal

By purchasing you expressly consented to immediate delivery of the digital content and acknowledged that you lose the statutory 14-day right of withdrawal once delivery has begun (Art. 16(m) of the EU Consumer Rights Directive and equivalent UK law). The voluntary guarantee above applies instead, and is more generous in one respect: it covers content you have already used. Your mandatory statutory rights in relation to defective digital content are unaffected.

6. Cancellation

The Course is a single one-time purchase. There is no subscription, nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel. Access does not expire, and future modules are included at no extra cost.

7. Before you dispute a charge

If you do not recognise a charge, or you are unhappy with your purchase, email contact@kczco.com first. Nearly everything is resolved within one working day, which is faster than any bank dispute. Chargebacks filed without contacting me first, for content that was delivered and accessed, are contested with the delivery and access records and result in permanent loss of access.

8. How to request a refund

Write to contact@kczco.com from the email you used at checkout, with the subject "Refund request", your purchase date, and the work described in section 2. Receipt is confirmed within one working day and a decision given within three.
